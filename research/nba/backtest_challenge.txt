NBA "$10 -> goal" challenge backtest (tools/nba/backtest_challenge.py). Seasons 2023-24 + 2024-25 pooled.
Every game night is a start date (step 1), 315 runs per row (165 nights/season less the last 8); runs are overlapping windows, so they are correlated.
Window = calendar nights from the start (owner cap 5; 3 and 7 shown for S0/S1). A run not finished in its window = not finished.
Price = fair x 0.95 per leg; same-game multi pays product x 0.965; cross-game pays the full product. Model v3 chances, keep=True, stage_x slack 2%, per_night=1.
S0 next game night; S1 skip unless tonight is in the best `stages left` of the window's nights (9-night forecast, nba_challenge.strength); S2 skip nights under 5 games (S1/S2 must play once no spare night is left).
fin% = every stage won; used = mean calendar nights used (window if unfinished); pred% = product of picked chances (0 if no multi reached the stage payout in the window).
multi mode: stack_for finds NO stack for big stage payouts (x of about 4+) -> pred 0, used = window. That is the search's limit (max 10 legs, legs >= 50%), not bad luck.

Conclusions (95% ranges are unpaired binomial, rough; overlapping runs make them a little wider)
1. Mode: game (same-game) beats multi. W5 S0 $250: 2.5% vs 0.6% finish (+-0.9, +-0.4); $1000 both under 0.5%. Multi finds no stack for high payouts.
2. Stages: 2 is best ($250 game W5 S0 4.1%, pred 2.1%; 3 stages 1.3%, 4 3.2%, 5 1.3%); model pred falls 2.1 -> 1.0% by 5 stages. $1000 is under 1% at any count: not realistic.
3. Window: 3 nights loses nothing vs 5 or 7 for 2-3 stages (game S0 $250, 2 stages: 4.1% at 3, 5 and 7 nights, used 1.2 nights); a longer window only helps skipping strategies.
4. S1/S2 vs S0 (game, W5, both goals): 1.59% / 1.67% vs 1.47%, i.e. +0.1 / +0.2 pt, 95% range about -0.7 to +0.9: within noise. S1 wins only at W7 2 stages ($250: 6.0% vs 4.1%, +-2.7), also noise.
5. Calibration: actual finish roughly matches or exceeds pred (2 stages $250: 4.1% vs 2.1%, 13 vs ~6.6 runs, p about 0.03); $1000 0.5% vs 0.4% matches. Expect about 2-4% for $250 in 2 stages, not more.
