3 · Odds-range ladder

Idea The five odds ranges are the rungs of a price ladder. Bar length is the price, so the trade-off between price and chance is visible.

Spatial system left axis rail, bars growing right from it, rungs ordered by price; sport page = same rail with over/under split bars.

Type / roles Inter 800 inside the bars for price, ring at the bar end per lock; kick-off as an amber time chip.

Visual risk price bars as the main graphic: long bar = big price = small ring.

Primary path scan rungs, pick the price you want, read its time chip; “bet first” tagged.

Empty / error a rung with no qualifying multi shows a faint empty rung: “Nothing clears the price in this range”.
