How the picks and figures work

Most likely is the runner with the highest chance of winning, and the one with the highest chance of placing, whatever their price. Value picks are made only when a price beats the runner's chance by enough, so many races have none. Each way counts as landed when the runner placed.

Confidence comes from the most likely winner's chance: High at 40% or more, Medium at 30% to 40%, Low under 30%. On races the model was not fitted on, High picks won about half the time, Medium about a third and Low about a quarter.

Per $1 is profit or loss per dollar staked at the prices shown. Saved races use starting prices, with place prices estimated from them (the place market's measured 40% margin included); exotic dividends were not saved. Those races were also used to fit the model, which flatters them a little: races from Derby Day on are the clean test.
